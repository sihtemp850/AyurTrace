Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit my herb harvest?

To submit your herb harvest, go to the "Herb Submissions" section and click "Submit Herbs". Fill in the required details including herb type, weight, grade, and GPS location. Make sure to capture environmental data for better traceability.

How are market rates determined?

Market rates are updated in real-time based on demand, quality grades, and market conditions. You can view current rates and trends in the "Market Rate Analytics" section.

What certifications do I need?

For medicinal herbs, you need Organic Certification and AYUSH approval. Upload your certificates in the profile section to get better rates and access to premium buyers.

How do I track my earnings?

Your earnings are automatically calculated and displayed in the Monthly Earnings Dashboard. You can view herb-wise breakdown, profit margins, and export detailed reports.
